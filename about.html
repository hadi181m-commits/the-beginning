About

Who we are and why we started The Beginning.

The Beginning started the way most obsessions do — with a single spool of filament, a printer pulled fresh out of the box, and a first print that came out warped, stringy, and nothing like the picture on the box.

That first failure led to another, and another after that. Late nights were spent chasing down layer shifts, re-leveling beds that refused to stay level, and falling down forum threads at 2am trying to understand why a perfectly good model kept turning into a pile of spaghetti. Slowly, the failures turned into small wins. The small wins turned into finished parts. And the finished parts turned into a genuine love for the craft — the strange satisfaction of watching an idea turn into something you can hold in your hand, layer by layer.

"Every expert was once a beginner who refused to stop printing."

The Beginning exists because that early struggle didn't have to be so hard. It was built by a 3D printing enthusiast — not a faceless company — who spent years learning the hard way and wanted to hand the next generation of makers a shorter, clearer path. Every module, every tier, and every project in this course comes from real trial and error, distilled into lessons that actually make sense the first time you read them.

This course isn't just about learning to run a printer. It's about becoming the kind of person who can look at a problem, design a solution, and print it into existence. Whether you're printing your first calibration cube or your fifteenth production run, The Beginning is here to take you from curious beginner to confident, capable 3D printing expert — one layer at a time.
